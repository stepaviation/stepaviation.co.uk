Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Draft for owner review

This policy reflects the confirmed enquiry-only purpose, Formspree Free plan and use of Google Workspace. The lawful-basis assessment, deletion process, international transfer safeguards and other details marked below still require confirmation before this policy is published as a final notice.

Step Aviation Ltd is responsible for deciding how information submitted through our enquiry form is used to handle enquiries. In that role, we are the data controller.

2. Information we collect

When you contact us, the form collects your full name, email address, telephone number including country code, company name if supplied, message and any other personal information you choose to include. Please provide only information relevant to your enquiry and avoid sensitive personal information.

Most information comes directly from you. If someone contacts us on another person's behalf, we may receive that person's contact details and information in the message from the sender. Where required by Article 14 of the UK GDPR, we will provide the person with privacy information within one month, or earlier at our first communication or disclosure, unless a relevant exception applies.

Technical information is processed separately from the form fields. GitHub Pages, which hosts this website, logs visitors' IP addresses for security. Formspree's privacy policy describes processing such as IP addresses, browser details, access times and referring websites to operate and protect its service.

3. How we use enquiry information

We use personal information submitted through our contact form only to review and respond to enquiries, communicate with prospective customers, and arrange or discuss requested aircraft repair and maintenance services. We do not use contact form information for unsolicited marketing.

We only contact you in connection with an enquiry you have submitted or subsequent communications about services you have requested. We do not use submitted contact details for unsolicited sales calls, promotional emails, marketing campaigns, newsletters or proactive offers of services.

4. Our lawful basis

Owner confirmation required before publication: the following bases are proposed for this enquiry process, not a verified record of the company's assessment.

  • Business enquiries: Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interests in responding to requested business communications and discussing our services, subject to assessing necessity and balancing those interests against your rights.
  • Pre-contract enquiries: Article 6(1)(b) may apply where you personally ask us to take steps before entering into a contract with you. It does not automatically cover a contact acting for a separate company.
  • Legal duties: Article 6(1)(c) applies only where a specific legal obligation requires the processing. Any applicable obligation and records must be identified.

Sending a message is not, by itself, treated as consent under UK GDPR. Before finalising this notice, we must confirm the basis for each purpose and document any legitimate interests assessment.

5. Formspree and other recipients

The form sends your information over HTTPS to Formspree, our external form-processing provider. Formspree processes and stores submissions on its infrastructure. Authorised Step Aviation personnel may access submissions through its dashboard. Depending on our account settings, Formspree may also send submission information by email to our configured mailbox.

We use Google Workspace (Gmail) to receive form notifications and communicate with customers. Google processes that correspondence as our email service provider. GitHub processes website hosting information. Other disclosures are limited to a valid business or legal reason, for example necessary professional advice or a lawful request from an authority.

Owner confirmation: check the authorised notification recipients, any enabled integrations or further recipients, access restrictions and applicable processing agreements.

6. How long information is kept

Formspree archive: we use Formspree's Free plan, which provides a 30-day submission archive. Our intended policy for the form submission archive is no longer than 30 calendar days after submission. Archive expiry is not evidence of permanent deletion from every Formspree system, including logs and backups.

Google Workspace correspondence: notification emails and enquiry correspondence are separate copies. We retain them for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, discuss requested services, maintain relevant business correspondence and comply with applicable legal obligations. We periodically review correspondence and delete personal information when it is no longer needed. Relevant criteria include whether the enquiry or requested service is still active, whether the record remains necessary to understand that correspondence, and whether a specific legal obligation requires retention. Gmail copies are not automatically deleted after 30 days.

Owner action: verify Formspree's archive expiry and permanent-deletion process, including backups, and arrange manual deletion where needed to meet the intended archive schedule. Set a documented review interval and appropriate deletion deadlines for Google Workspace correspondence, exports and any service or legally required records. Identify any relevant legal duties and retention periods. This website cannot delete provider-held records, and no automatic permanent erasure after exactly 30 days is promised.

7. International transfers

Formspree states that its services are hosted with Amazon Web Services in the United States and that it relies on Standard Contractual Clauses as a processor. This establishes that processing may take place outside the UK; it does not verify the agreements covering our account.

Google's Cloud Data Processing Addendum describes international processing and transfer provisions. Google Workspace and GitHub may process information outside the UK; this notice does not assume UK-only storage.

Owner confirmation required: verify the Formspree processing agreement and the UK transfer mechanism that actually applies, including any necessary UK Addendum or other safeguard and assessment. Check the agreements and settings applicable to Google Workspace and GitHub too. Confirm how individuals can obtain a copy of the applicable safeguards through our contact email. Published provider terms do not establish which agreements or transfer arrangements cover this account.

8. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, you may have rights to be informed, access your information, correct inaccurate information, request erasure or restrict processing. You may also have rights relating to significant automated decisions, including asking for human intervention and challenging a decision where applicable.

Right to object: you may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation. We must consider that objection under the applicable rules.

Data portability may apply to information you provided where processing is automated and based on consent or a contract. It does not apply to every enquiry record. If we ever rely on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

To make a request, contact a.stepanovs@stepaviation.co.uk. We may need information to verify your identity. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

9. Security

Our approach is to use appropriate safeguards for enquiry information. The public website uses HTTPS, and the form sends submissions to Formspree over HTTPS. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Owner confirmation: verify the company's practical safeguards, including authorised access, account security and the handling of email copies. This notice does not certify internal security controls.

10. Cookies, analytics and security tools

The reviewed website code does not set cookies or use browser storage, analytics, marketing pixels, embedded third-party content or embedded CAPTCHA scripts. Images, fonts and scripts are served locally or provided by the device. The form connects to Formspree when submitted.

This describes the website code, not every provider's systems. Hosting security logs and Formspree's server-side anti-spam processing are separate. A provider-hosted confirmation or security challenge can have its own technologies and privacy information.

Owner confirmation: review enabled Formspree anti-spam tools and other account integrations, including any challenge presented to visitors. Check their actual cookie or storage use and any applicable consent requirement before enabling non-essential technologies. No cookie consent banner has been added for technologies absent from the reviewed code.

11. Your choice to provide information

You are not generally legally required to submit an enquiry. The form requires your name, email address, telephone number and message to receive and respond to your request through this channel. Company name is optional. If you leave required fields incomplete, the form cannot submit; you can contact us directly by email instead.

Owner confirmation: review whether a mandatory telephone number is necessary for every enquiry.

12. Automated decisions

The reviewed website validates form fields and submits enquiries; it does not make decisions about individuals with legal or similarly significant effects or carry out profiling for that purpose. Provider anti-spam tools may automatically filter messages.

Owner confirmation: confirm that no connected service or subsequent company process makes significant automated decisions using enquiry data.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its last updated date. Where required, we will provide information about a new use before that processing starts.
